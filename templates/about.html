Project Overview

This system performs early-stage screening of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) using structured clinical inputs. It applies supervised machine learning models to identify risk patterns that are often missed in early diagnosis.

Multiple models were evaluated, and a final Ensemble Model (Logistic Regression + Random Forest) was selected based on performance consistency and recall optimization.

The focus is on high recall, ensuring potential CKD cases are not missed — a critical requirement in medical screening systems.

Kidney Diagram / Model Visualization

System Strengths

  • Recall-Oriented Design: Prioritizes detection of risk cases to avoid missed diagnosis.
  • Ensemble Stability: Reduces model variance and improves generalization.
  • Clinical Relevance: Uses medically significant parameters aligned with CKD indicators.

CKD Overview

Chronic Kidney Disease is a progressive condition where kidney function declines over time, leading to toxin buildup and systemic health complications.

CKD is often asymptomatic in early stages, making data-driven screening essential for timely intervention.
